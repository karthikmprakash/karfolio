About

I combine software, hardware, big data and math to solve real-world problems — with a lot of care for architecture and detail.

I'm Karthik, a self-taught data scientist, software engineer and automation developer. I extract, transform and combine incoming data, hunting for complex patterns that surface a previously hidden insight — the kind that gives a business a competitive edge or fixes a pressing problem.

I'm also an open-source enthusiast and maintainer. I've learned a great deal from the community, and I love how collaboration and shared knowledge move everyone forward.

Away from the keyboard I'm a professional photographer, illustrator, calligraphist, aviation enthusiast and amateur ornithologist. Right now I'm looking to collaborate on a wireless UWB ECG electrode.
